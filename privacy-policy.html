Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

WorksAsExplained is owned and operated by Muhammad Zakria, based in Pakistan.

What this policy covers

This policy explains what information WorksAsExplained collects from visitors, how cookies are used, and how third-party advertising — including Google AdSense — may use data collected on this site.

Information we collect

We collect limited technical information automatically when you visit, such as browser type, device type, approximate location (derived from IP address), pages viewed, and referring site. If you use the contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message you provide.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them for:

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to function, such as remembering your cookie consent choice.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand which guides are useful and which pages need improvement.
  • Advertising cookies — set by Google and its advertising partners to serve and measure ads, and, where you've consented, to personalize the ads you see.

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies using the banner shown on this site, and you can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.

Google AdSense and advertising

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Depending on your location, you may also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

For more detail on how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see Google's "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" page.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site — which pages are read, how long visitors stay, and which sources bring people here. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information, which is processed by Google in accordance with its own privacy practices. You can learn more or opt out of Google Analytics tracking across sites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Your choices

  • Use the cookie banner to accept or reject non-essential cookies.
  • Adjust ad personalization at Google's Ads Settings.
  • Block or delete cookies through your browser's privacy settings.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our Contact page.
